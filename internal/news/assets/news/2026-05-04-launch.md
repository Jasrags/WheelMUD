# Launch checkpoint

WheelMUD now greets connections with a real splash screen, shows the
MOTD on character entry, and tracks per-character news watermarks.
The {{news}}::yellow command lists dated entries; reading one bumps
your watermark so reconnect summaries stay accurate.

Other landings this week: zone-wide {{shout}}::yellow / {{yell}}::yellow,
the admin {{zonemap}}::yellow renderer, sector-tinted player maps, and
auto-derived room coordinates for new builds.
