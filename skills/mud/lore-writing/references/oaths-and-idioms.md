# Oaths and idioms

Sourced palette. Use the right one for the right speaker; misplacing
an oath is the loudest voice rot in WoT prose.

## General westlands oaths

- "Light!" / "Light burn me!" / "Light burn my soul!" — surprise,
  frustration. Ubiquitous westlands.
- "Blood and ashes!" — crude oath. Common to soldiers, taverns,
  Two Rivers folk in distress.
- "Mother's milk in a cup!" — shock or dismay.
- "By the Light." / "May the Light shine on you." / "May the Light
  illumine you." — formal blessings; closing or opening greetings.
- "The Creator shelter us." — pious; older speakers, devout.
- "May you find water and shade." — Aiel-only; do not let outsiders
  use it.

## Honor formulas

- "On the honor of House <X>." — Cairhienin, Andoran nobles,
  Tairen High Lords.
- "I have toh." / "I see you." — Aiel, ji'e'toh.
- "Peace favor your sword." — Borderlander parting; heard at every
  Borderland inn.
- "May we both feed the hawks together." — older Borderlander
  formula (death before dishonor).

## Insults / contempt

- "Wetlander." — Aiel for any westlander.
- "Treekiller." — Aiel for Cairhienin (kin-cousin to Avi's family
  story; sharp).
- "Darkfriend." — universally horrific accusation; do not use
  lightly.
- "Soft." — Borderlander slur for southlanders who flinch from
  Trolloc raids.

## Faction-specific oaths

- "Under the Light, and by my hope of salvation and rebirth, I
  vow…" — Whitecloak formal vow.
- "By the Three Oaths." — Aes Sedai swearing; the oath rod is
  literal; rare to invoke.
- "Saidar willing." / "Saidin take you." — channeler-flavored
  asides.

## Common civilian idioms

- "Wool-headed." — affectionate frustration (Two Rivers especially).
- "The hen rules the roost." — Borderlander acknowledgment of a
  fierce wife.
- "Like riding a rooftop in a thunderstorm." — danger.
- "A man's word is his bond, his sword his honor, his hand his
  promise." — Borderland creed.

## Don't

- Do not import Tolkien-isms ("by my beard", "yea, verily").
- Do not import modern idioms ("on the same page", "out of the
  box", "win-win").
- Do not have a Whitecloak swear "blood and ashes" — too casual for
  their voice.
- Do not have an Aiel use "Light burn me" — they speak of "He Who
  Comes With the Dawn" and the threefold land, not Light-oaths.
