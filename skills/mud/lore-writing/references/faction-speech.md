# Faction speech

Faction overrides nation when they conflict. An Andoran Aes Sedai
speaks Aes Sedai first.

## Aes Sedai

- Three Oaths shape every line: cannot speak a word that is not
  true, cannot use the Power as a weapon, cannot make weapons. The
  first oath produces baroque hedging — no plain lies, but artful
  near-truths and weighted silences.
- Voice: composed, layered, faintly imperious. Ajah colors lurk in
  word choice (Red distrustful of male channelers, Blue passionate
  about causes, Green plainspoken-warlike, Yellow brisk and
  practical, Brown scholarly, White detached, Gray diplomatic).
- Sample: "It is not impossible that what you ask might be
  considered, given time and the proper offering."

## Whitecloaks (Children of the Light)

- Zealous, certain, contemptuous of Aes Sedai (Darkfriends in their
  view), wary of channeling, blunt with civilians, polite with
  authority.
- Voice: declarative, moralistic. "The Light burns away the
  darkness." Ranks matter — Lord Captain, Inquisitor — and they
  sneer at "sworn" titles.
- Sample: "Step aside, citizen. The Children of the Light have
  business that does not concern you."

## Asha'man

- Black-coated male channelers. Clipped, command-voice — they were
  taught one way to speak under Taim. "Asha'man, kill" is the
  literal training cadence.
- Voice: short sentences, no softening. Saidin's edge bleeds through.
- Sample: "You will leave. Now."

## Forsaken

- Ageless, disdainful, velvet-menace. Spent millennia in the Bore;
  modern speech sounds quaint to them.
- Voice: archaic flourishes, condescension, faint pity for humans.
- Sample: "Such ambition for so small a creature. Run along,
  little one. Your master has not yet finished with you."

## Wise Ones

- Aiel female counterparts to Aes Sedai (some channel, some don't).
- Voice: blunt as desert wind. Treat ji'e'toh as natural law. Will
  cuff a sister or queen alike if she's earned it.
- Sample: "You are wrong. Sit. Drink water. Then we will talk
  about why you are wrong."

## Warders

- Bonded to Aes Sedai. Quiet, deadly, unobtrusive in a room.
- Voice: minimal. Speak when asked; observe when not.
- Sample: (silence, then) "She will see you now."

## Forsaken-adjacent — Black Ajah

- Aes Sedai forsworn — same composure, masked menace, free of the
  three oaths so they CAN lie. The lie itself is part of the threat.

## Children of the Dragon / Aiel of the Stone

- After the Dragon is proclaimed: a new register. Honor carried
  forward, awe carried for the Car'a'carn.
