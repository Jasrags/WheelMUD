# Nation flavor

One-line tells per nation, then a sample line per nation. Use the
sample as a calibration target, not a copy.

## Andor

- Formal but warm. Queens and queens-to-be. Two Rivers folk are
  technically Andoran but speak with a distinct rural cadence.
- Sample: "Welcome to the Queen's Blessing, friend. You'll find
  the ale honest and the rooms quieter than the night."

## Cairhien

- Daes Dae'mar — Game of Houses. Layered meaning; nothing said
  plainly. Composure prized; shaved foreheads on nobles.
- Sample: "Of course you are welcome here. I cannot imagine why
  you would think otherwise — particularly given your obvious...
  qualities."

## Tear

- Pride, formality, contempt for "wetlander" custom by other Tairens
  and contempt for *foreign* wetlanders by Tairens themselves. High
  Lords scheme; commoners are blunt.
- Sample: "You'll keep your sword belted in this house, outlander,
  or you'll keep it outside."

## Illian

- Distinctive grammar — present continuous and dropped articles. "We
  are knowing what to be doing." Pride in the city, the Council of
  Nine, the Great Blackwood.
- Sample: "Be welcome, then. We are having ale, and we are not
  charging foreigners more than the locals — not by much."

## Saldaea

- Warmth and fire. Saldaean women are notoriously fierce; the
  Borderlands fight Trollocs constantly so death-talk is matter of
  fact.
- Sample: "If you bring trouble through that door, friend, my wife
  will see to you before the Trollocs do, and she'll be the more
  thorough."

## Aiel

- No idle words. Direct to the point of abruptness; honor (ji'e'toh)
  in every transaction. Concept of "wetlander" used flatly.
- Sample: "I see you. You wish water? Drink. We will speak after."

## Sea Folk (Atha'an Miere)

- Bargaining cadence — every interaction is a port-and-bargain.
  Tattooed; nose chains for Wavemistresses. Warm to those they trust;
  formal to all others.
- Sample: "Come aboard, then, and we will bargain. The price of
  passage we shall set; the price of welcome you have earned."

## Domani

- Indirect, sensual, mercantile. Domani women's reputation is half
  legend and half wielded on purpose.
- Sample: "I do not haggle, traveler. I let you offer what you
  think the silk is worth, and we discover together whether you
  are right."

## Tar Valon (city, not faction)

- Aes Sedai live here; non-channelers in town speak with reverence
  toward sisters and a slight wariness toward all things tower.
- Sample: "Mind your tongue near a sister, and mind your purse
  near anyone else, and you'll do well enough in Tar Valon."

## Borderlanders (Shienar / Arafel / Kandor / Saldaea collectively)

- Laconic warmth. Welcome strangers; have no time for bullshit;
  cannot bear cowardice.
- Sample: "Peace favor your sword."
